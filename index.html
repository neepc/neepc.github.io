About the conference

By PhD students, for PhD students

NEEPC brings together economics PhD students from across New England to present their research, get feedback from peers and faculty, and meet the people working on similar questions at nearby schools.

The conference is organized by the Graduate Student Association of the Department of Economics at Brown University. All fields of economics are welcome, and students at every stage of the PhD are encouraged to submit, from early work in progress to job market papers.
